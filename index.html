Why Yahoo’s numbers differ from recent results

Jaylin Noel (WR, HOU)Yahoo 7.1 vs 12.9 so far
Houston traded for Marvin Harrison Jr. (WR) on Oct 13, which analysts expect to cut Noel’s targets. Nico Collins (WR) is back as the WR1 and Tank Dell (WR) is nearing a return. Noel plays only 26% to 62% of snaps. His projection could drop further, or hold if Harrison’s role stays small.
Malik Washington (WR, MIA)Yahoo 10.3 vs 10.5
Miami released Tyreek Hill and traded Jaylen Waddle, so he’s the top wideout, and he returns kicks, which your league scores. Risk: rookie Caleb Douglas (WR) may return from an ankle injury this week, and Washington scored 8.7 PPR points or less in the first two games Douglas played.
Pat Freiermuth (TE, PIT)Yahoo 8.1
Steelers receiver injuries pushed his targets up, and his next opponents (New Orleans, Cleveland) allow a lot of TE points. Risk: four TDs on 21 catches, and Darnell Washington (TE) shares the work.
Jake Ferguson (TE, DAL)Yahoo 5.7 vs 3.3 median
He has been out-targeted by WR3 Ryan Flournoy in five of six games and is the fourth option behind CeeDee Lamb (WR) and George Pickens (WR). Yahoo’s number probably still reflects last year’s role (82 catches, 8 TDs).
Saquon Barkley (RB, PHI)Yahoo 12.7 vs 5.2
He left Week 4 in the first quarter and has missed two games with a hamstring injury, which drags his average down. Yahoo assumes a heavy workload: Jalen Hurts (QB) is out for the season, the other Eagles backs are on injured reserve, and Dallas has allowed 842 rushing yards in five games. Risks: his hamstring, a weaker offense with Tanner McKee (QB), and a Monday-night game you can’t swap out of.
Cam Skattebo (RB, NYG)Yahoo 5.1
He got zero touches on six snaps in Week 6 after four fumbles in five games, so his season line now counts that game as 0. The Giants released Najee Harris (RB), which opens the backfield, but analysts say not to trust him until the coach uses him again, and Houston is a hard matchup.
Devin Lloyd (LB, CAR)Yahoo 8.9 vs 14.0
One 31-point game inflates his average, and he missed a game with a calf injury. ESPN’s Ben Solak expected him to fall back from last year’s career season, and Carolina has less talent around him than Jacksonville did.
Rachaad White (RB, WAS)Yahoo 6.8 vs 10.3
He’s the passing-downs back, but Jacory Croskey-Merritt (RB) is the lead back, and White missed Week 4 with a shoulder injury. About 22 of his 52 points came from two TDs and kick returns, and Yahoo expects the returns to fade.
