Price vs benefit

The bid is the lower of that and what it likely takes to beat the other teams. When beating a rival would cost more than the player is worth, bid enough to beat everyone else and let that rival have him.

Rest of season: spend now or save?

You’re 5-1, but 8th of 10 in points scored123.1 vs 130.0 avg
You’ve also had the fewest points scored against you in the league (690.6), so part of the record is schedule luck. To stay on top, and especially for playoff weeks, the starting lineup needs to score more. The weakest starting spots are RB2 (Barkley’s hamstring, Marks at about 9 a game) and the flex.
Why spend a little now
This week’s claims cost about $17 and leave about $66. That’s enough for two or three big moves later: in this league a starting RB or WR has gone for $10 to $25. Waiting wouldn’t get you these players cheaper. White is cheapest now, while he’s on bye. Washington goes to someone else if you skip him. Freiermuth is also cheapest now: no team bids on TEs.
What to save the rest for
A real RB2 upgrade (a lead back after an injury elsewhere), an injury to one of your starters, and playoff weeks. When one of those comes up, bid what it’s worth. Those are the claims where $10 to $20 makes sense.

How this league bids

Winning bids this season
Defenses: $0 to $4, once $10. LBs: $0 to $3. TEs: one bid all season ($10, ours, for Ferguson). WRs: $5 to $25, usually $10 to $19. These are every winning bid of the season: each team’s spending adds up exactly to its remaining budget.
Who actually bids
team name pending ($28 left) and Jack Russell ($24 left) spend the most. Concussed Tim Tebow ($60) usually bids $5 or $10. Rude Rudy and PaytonsRogerPeePants have $100 each and have never bid; VT FCS! ($96) has never gone above $3. Big budgets here don’t mean big bids.
Who needs what this week
Bids here look need-driven. Jack Russell needs a WR (Davante Adams out, DJ Moore and Keon Coleman on bye) and is the most active team in the league, with $24 left. They’re the likeliest rival for Washington, but they may add a free agent for this week instead. team name pending needs a QB (Josh Allen on bye, no backup), which cuts into what they can spend on Washington. Tebow needs a defensive player (Greg Rousseau on bye), so he may bid on Lloyd. Green and VT FCS! need kickers. Nobody has a TE or defense on bye, and nobody needs White this week because he’s on bye.
