Rest of season: spend now or save?

You’re 5-1, but 8th of 10 in points scored123.1 vs 130.0 avg
You’ve also had the fewest points scored against you in the league (690.6), so part of the record is schedule luck. To stay on top, and especially for playoff weeks, the starting lineup needs to score more. The weakest starting spots are RB2 (Barkley’s hamstring, Marks at about 9 a game) and the flex.
Why spend a little now
This week’s claims cost about $17 and leave about $66. That’s enough for two or three big moves later: in this league a starting RB or WR has gone for $10 to $25. Waiting wouldn’t get you these players cheaper. White is cheapest now, while he’s on bye. Washington goes to someone else if you skip him. Freiermuth is also cheapest now: no team bids on TEs.
What to save the rest for
A real RB2 upgrade (a lead back after an injury elsewhere), an injury to one of your starters, and playoff weeks. When one of those comes up, bid what it’s worth. Those are the claims where $10 to $20 makes sense.
